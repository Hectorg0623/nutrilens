Know what's really in your food.

NutriLens teaches you what to eat — not just what to avoid. Point it at any food and it explains what's good, what's harmful, why it matters for your body, and the better real-food swaps to reach for instead.

Food Analyzer

🔥 Powered by Firecrawl

Paste a link to any food or grocery product page. NutriLens uses Firecrawl to scrape the page and run an LLM extraction against a custom nutrition schema — turning a messy web page into structured health data in one call.

Try an example: Coca-Cola · Nutella · Frosted Flakes · Rolled Oats

NutriLens is an educational prototype and does not provide medical advice. Nutrition figures are extracted from third-party product pages via Firecrawl and may be incomplete. Always check the physical label and consult a professional for dietary decisions.
